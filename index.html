FIELD SCOPE

What we walk, climb, and check.

Every inspection follows the same disciplined checklist — nothing skipped because it was hard to reach.

B1

Roof & Attic

Shingles, flashing, ventilation, and visible insulation levels.

B2

Foundation & Structure

Cracking, settlement, and moisture intrusion at the foundation walls.

B3

Electrical Panel & Wiring

Panel condition, visible wiring type, and obvious code concerns.

B4

Plumbing & Water Heater

Supply lines, drainage, fixtures, and water heater age/condition.

B5

HVAC Systems

Furnace or heat pump, central air, and ductwork where accessible.

B6

Basement & Crawlspace

Moisture, pest evidence, and structural support members.
